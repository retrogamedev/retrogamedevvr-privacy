Privacy Policy - RetroGameDev VR

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the RetroGameDev VR application ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. The app is developed and published by RetroGameDev. We have designed the app to keep your data on your own device wherever possible.

The short version

RetroGameDev VR does not transmit, sell, or share any personal information about you. We have no servers that receive your data, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no user accounts. Everything you create or load stays on your headset. The one category of personal data the app does process is your Meta user ID and your purchase status, read on your headset through Meta's platform services to verify ownership of the app and its add-on content. This is described in full under "Data accessed through Meta platform services" below, together with how to have it deleted.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your Meta Quest headset, never transmitted to us:

  • Games, projects, and edits you create with the built-in editors.
  • Game files, save data, and video files you choose to load.
  • App settings and preferences.
  • Any password you set for the optional local file-transfer feature (stored on the headset only).

This data is removed if you uninstall the app, subject to the platform's standard behaviour.

Network connections

The app makes a limited number of network connections, none of which send personal information about you:

  • Optional emulator core downloads. If you choose to download an emulator core, the app requests the file from our public software repository hosted on GitHub. This is a standard file download. Delivery infrastructure provided by GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address as part of serving the file; we do not receive, store, or have access to that information.
  • Local file transfer. The app can run a temporary web server on your headset so you can transfer your own game files and saves to and from another device on your own local network (for example, your home computer). This connection stays within your local network and does not reach us or any third party.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no crash-reporting that sends data to us.

Data sharing and sale

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any user information. The only personal data the app processes (described in the next section) is exchanged between your headset and Meta's own services and never reaches us.

Data accessed through Meta platform services

RetroGameDev VR is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. Your purchase, your Meta account, and your device are managed by Meta under Meta's own privacy policy, which is separate from this one and outside our control.

The app uses the Meta Platform SDK on your headset to access the following data about you:

  • Meta user ID. The identifier of the Meta account signed in on the headset. What it is used for: to ask Meta's services whether that account obtained the app from the Store and whether it owns any add-on content (such as the Arcade Room). How it is collected: the app requests it from the Meta Platform SDK when it runs these checks. Storage: it is used in memory for the duration of the check only. The app does not write it to disk, and it is never transmitted to us or to anyone other than Meta.
  • Purchase and entitlement status. Whether your Meta account owns the app, which add-ons it owns, and whether an add-on's download file is installed on the headset. What it is used for: to unlock add-on content you have purchased, to download that content to your headset from Meta's servers, and to refuse add-on content to an account that has not purchased it. How it is collected: returned by the Meta Platform SDK in response to the checks above. Storage: once an add-on has been verified as owned, the app keeps a single small record on your headset (a code derived from your device and the add-on, not your user ID) so it does not need to repeat the check. Nothing is transmitted to us.

This data is used solely for the purposes above. It is not used for analytics, advertising, profiling, or any other purpose, and it is not combined with any other data. It is retained on your headset only, until you uninstall the app or request its deletion as described below. Meta's handling of your account and purchase records is governed by Meta's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete all data the app holds about you at any time:

  • On the headset: uninstalling RetroGameDev VR removes everything the app has stored, including your projects, settings, and the add-on ownership record described above. Within the app, the ownership record is also removed when you clear the app's storage from the headset's Settings.
  • By request: email contact@retrogamedev.com with "Data deletion request" in the subject line. Because we hold no personal data on any server, we will confirm this in writing within 30 days and help you remove any on-device data if needed.
  • Your Meta account and purchase history are held by Meta, not by us, and are managed through your Meta account settings under Meta's privacy policy.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Your rights

You have the right to know what personal data the app processes (set out above), to have it deleted (see "Deleting your data"), and to ask us questions about it. We hold no personal data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to export or correct; the content and records on your headset remain under your control. If you have questions about your rights under the UK GDPR, EU GDPR, or other privacy laws, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
